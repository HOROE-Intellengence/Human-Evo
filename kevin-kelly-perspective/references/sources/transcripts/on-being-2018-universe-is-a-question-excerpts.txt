Status: 一手 (first-hand) — On Being publishes an official transcript on its
own site. Fragments below are the verbatim quotations recovered via research
tooling; this is a curated excerpt, not the full transcript.

ON TECHNOLOGY AND MORAL OBLIGATION:
"We have a moral obligation to increase the amount of technology in the
world, the amount of possibilities."

ON TECHNOLOGY'S SPIRITUAL DIMENSION:
"I believe that technology is a divine force and looking at the spiritual
dimensions would be fantastic."
"Technology is a way of generating surprises. That's the spiritual
dimension of technology."

ON MEANING / THE TITLE THESIS:
"It's very likely that the universe is really a kind of a question, rather
than the answer to anything."

ON SELF-CORRECTION IN REAL TIME (re: a quotation attributed to Picasso that
he initially was unsure about):
"I actually did some research, and I found out he actually did say that in
the 1960s."

ON REVERSING A PREVIOUSLY STATED PUBLIC POSITION (re: whether he uses a
smartphone, having previously said or implied he did not):
"No, that's not true. That was true then, but no, I have a laptop. I do
have a smartphone."

ON PARENTING AND TECHNOLOGY, ADMITTING UNCERTAINTY RATHER THAN OFFERING
A CONFIDENT ANSWER:
"I don't even know right now."
