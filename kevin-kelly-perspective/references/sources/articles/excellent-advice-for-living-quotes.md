## From themarginalian.org (full sentences, high confidence)
1. "Listening well is a superpower. While listening to someone you love keep asking them 'Is there more?' until there is no more."
2. "When you forgive others they may not notice but you will heal. Forgiveness is not something we do for others; it is a gift to ourselves."
3. "Forgiveness is accepting the apology you will never get."
4. "How to apologize: quickly, specifically, sincerely. Don't ruin an apology with an excuse."
5. "A proper apology consists of conveying the 3 Rs: regret (genuine empathy with the other) responsibility (not blaming someone else) and remedy (your willingness to fix it)."
6. "Whenever you have a choice between being right or being kind be kind. No exceptions. Don't confuse kindness with weakness."
7. "Anger is not the proper response to anger. When you see someone angry you are seeing their pain. Compassion is the proper response to anger."
8. "Don't measure your life with someone else's ruler."
9. "The greatest killer of happiness is comparison. If you must compare, compare yourself to you yesterday."
10. "Habit is far more dependable than inspiration. Make progress by making habits. Don't focus on getting into shape. Focus on becoming the kind of person who never misses a workout."
11. "Keep showing up. 99% of success is just showing up. In fact, most success is just persistence."
12. "Before you are old attend as many funerals as you can bear and listen. Nobody talks about the departed's achievements. The only thing people will remember is what kind of person you were while you were achieving."
13. "If you are the smartest person in the room, you are in the wrong room. Hang out with, and learn from people smarter than yourself. Even better, find smart people who will disagree with you."
14. "Promptness is a sign of respect."
15. "Your passions should fit you exactly but your purpose in life should exceed you. Work for something much larger than yourself."
16. "Bad things can happen fast but almost all good things happen slowly."
17. "The best work ethic requires a good rest ethic."
18. "Criticize in private, praise in public."
19. "You will thrive more — and so will others — when you promote what you love rather than bash what you hate. Life is short; focus on the good stuff."
20. "The chief prevention against getting old is to remain astonished."

## From stairway.highexistence.com (full sentences, high confidence)
21. "Over the long term, the future is decided by optimists. To be an optimist, you don't have to ignore the multitude of problems we create; you just have to imagine how much our ability to solve problems improves."
22. "We tend to overestimate what we can do in a day, and underestimate what we can achieve in a decade. Miraculous things can be accomplished if you give it 10 years. A long game will compound small gains that will be able to overcome even big mistakes."
23. "The advantage of a ridiculously ambitious goal is that it sets the bar very high, so even if your effort falls short, it may exceed an ordinary success."
24. "When you feel like quitting, just do five more: 5 more minutes, 5 more pages, 5 more steps. Then repeat. Sometimes, you can break through and keep going, but even if you can't, you ended five ahead. Tell yourself that you will quit tomorrow, but not today."
25. "When you give away 10% of your income, you lose 10% of your purchasing power, which is minor compared to the 110% increase in happiness you will gain."
26. "The more you are interested in others the more interesting they'll find you. To be interesting, be interested."
27. "The best way to learn anything is to try to teach what you know."
28. "When you are young, spend at least 6 months to 1 year living as cheaply as you can, owning as little as you possibly can, eating beans and rice in a tiny room or tent. That way, any time you have to risk something in the future, you won't be afraid of the 'worst-case' scenario."
29. "To succeed, get other people to pay you; to become wealthy, help other people to succeed."
30. "Whenever there is an argument between two sides, find the third side."
31. "Go with the option that opens up yet more options."
32. "Reading to your children regularly is the best school they will ever get."
33. "Read the books that your favorite authors once read."
34. "The easy choice pays off right away. The best choice will pay off at the end."
35. "Embrace pronoia, which is the opposite of paranoia. Choose to believe that the entire universe is conspiring behind your back to make you a success."

## From medium.com/illumination (full sentences, high confidence)
36. "A great way to understand yourself is to seriously reflect on everything you find irritating in others."
37. "You can obsess about your customers or you can obsess about beating the competition. Both work, but of the two obsessing about your customers will take you further."
38. "Separate the processes of creating from improving. You can't write and edit or sculpt and polish or make and analyze at the same time. If you do, the editor stops the creator. While you invent, don't select. While you sketch, don't inspect. While you write the first draft, don't reflect. At the start, the creator mind must be unleashed from judgment."
